# Afterimage submission draft

Learn to move a suspended star, then study and rebuild three constellations from memory. Pinch Check to see the correct outlines, the distance from each placement to its target, and your accuracy. The final score averages the three rounds.

## Test on Quest

When a headset is available, please execute these checks and record its model, Quest Browser version, build identifier and results in STATUS.md.

1. Open the authorized HTTPS build in Quest Browser while seated, with hand tracking enabled and controllers set aside. Select **Enter VR** with a hand and grant any requested permission.
2. Face forward. Confirm the board appears within comfortable reach and stays in place when you turn your head.
3. With the right hand, pinch the gold star, move it into the outline and release. Confirm round one begins and the five-second countdown hides the constellation. Rebuild using both hands in turn and verify each released star remains suspended. Pinch Check, inspect the three outlines and error lines, and pinch Next. Complete three rounds and pinch Play again.
4. Release outside the frame beyond the allowed volume. Confirm the star returns to its starting position. Move a held hand out of tracking and bring it back. Check for an unexpected match or a stuck grab.
5. Remove and replace the headset. Exit VR using hands and enter again. Confirm the tutorial resets and still accepts a grab.
6. Measure frame rate on the headset during grabs and releases. Record the tool, sample duration and observed range.
